An old recipe,
set on a new table.

We bring the age-old traditional mithai to a modern table — flavours that remind you of childhood, festivals and gatherings, handmade and reimagined for a modern palate.

Every laddoo is rolled by hand each morning, in small batches, the way a family kitchen would do it. Then the doors open at seven and the room fills — for filter coffee and a dosa, for chaat at four, for dinner under the copper lamps.
