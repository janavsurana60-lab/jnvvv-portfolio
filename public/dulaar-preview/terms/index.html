Food, allergens and dietary needs

The kitchen is entirely vegetarian. Beyond that, it is a single kitchen: milk, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame are all in constant use, and mithai in particular is made with ghee, khoya and nuts as a matter of course. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free of a given allergen, and we cannot promise separate preparation surfaces.

If you have an allergy, tell us in the note on your booking and tell the person who seats you. We would far rather steer you to something safe than have you find out the hard way.
